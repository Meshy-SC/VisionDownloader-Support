Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026 · Last updated: October 9, 2026

The short version

Vision Downloader collects nothing about you and sends nothing to us. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It reads the pages you visit only to find their videos, keeps your settings and download history in your browser, and talks only to the sites you download from. This page explains the details.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy covers the Vision Downloader browser extension. It does not cover the websites you visit with it, which have their own policies.

2. What the extension reads

  • The pages you visit, to find their videos: which requests a page makes for video, audio, playlists and subtitles; the headers the site's own player sends with them; and the page's title, its video poster and a few frames of the video that is playing, to name and preview each video in the list. This stays in your browser's memory while the tab is open, and is not written anywhere.

3. What it keeps, on your computer

  • Your settings.
  • A history of your downloads — file name, site, page address, quality, size and date — if the history is on. It is on by default and can be turned off or cleared in the settings. Downloads from private (incognito) windows are never added.
  • Downloads in progress, while they are being assembled.

All of this stays in your browser's own storage on your computer. Uninstalling the extension removes it.

4. What it sends, and to whom

  • To the site you download from: the requests needed to fetch the video you chose, made the way the site's own player makes them.
  • To us, or anyone else: nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reports and no update checks of our own. We do not sell, share or transfer any data, because we have none.

The extension knows a few things about particular sites — for example, that it must not download from YouTube — from a file of rules that ships inside it. This version fetches no updates to those rules. If a later version does, this policy will say where from before it does, and that request will carry no information about you or your browsing.

5. Why it asks for its permissions

Videos can be on any site, so the extension can see the pages you visit; it uses that only to find their videos and fetch the one you choose. Its other permissions save files to your Downloads folder, keep your settings and history, and add the right-click menu and the side panel. Notifications are asked for only if you turn them on.

6. Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

7. Your choices

Everything the extension keeps is on your computer and under your control: turn the history off or clear it in the settings, or remove the extension to delete everything it stored. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it here, with a new "Last updated" date, before the version of the extension it applies to is published.
