Vision Downloader

Finds the video on a page and saves it in the quality you pick.

A browser extension for Google Chrome. It lists the videos on the page you are watching — plain files and HLS and DASH streams — in every quality the site offers, and saves the one you choose as MP4, MKV or audio, with its subtitles. Free.
